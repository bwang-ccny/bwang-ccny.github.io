Autonomous Systems and Control Laboratory (ASCL)

 

The Autonomous Systems and Control Laboratory (ASCL) is part of the Department of Mechanical Engineering at The City College of New York. The laboratory conducts theoretical and experimental research in nonlinear control, robotics, and autonomous systems.

Our research seeks to develop rigorous and implementable control methods that enable robotic and autonomous systems to operate safely and effectively under nonlinear dynamics, limited actuation, sensing constraints, and interactions with other agents and the environment.

Current research interests include:

  • Safety-critical control and autonomous systems

  • Extremum seeking and source seeking

  • Nonholonomic and underactuated robotic systems

  • Networked and multi-agent autonomous systems

  • Nonlinear and distributed control

  • Learning- and optimization-based control

The laboratory is directed by Bo Wang, Assistant Professor of Mechanical Engineering at CCNY.

Laboratory

The Autonomous Systems and Control Laboratory provides experimental facilities for research and education in nonlinear control, robotics, and autonomous systems. The laboratory features a 12-camera Vicon motion capture system, Quanser control and mechatronics platforms, differential-drive mobile robots, and facilities for robotic prototyping and fabrication. The robotic platforms incorporate onboard computing and sensing technologies, including Raspberry Pi, Arduino systems, and LiDAR sensors, and are used for the implementation and experimental validation of control algorithms in areas such as motion control, source seeking, safety-critical control, navigation, and multi-robot coordination.
