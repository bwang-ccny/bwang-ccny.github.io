Biography

Bo Wang is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at The City College of New York. He received the M.S. degree in Control Theory and Engineering from the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences in 2018 and the Ph.D. degree in Mechanical Engineering from Villanova University in 2022. Prior to joining CCNY, he was a postdoctoral fellow at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. His research focuses on nonlinear control and autonomy, particularly safety-critical control, extremum and source seeking, and cooperative control of networked, nonholonomic, and underactuated robotic systems.

Research

  • Safety-Critical Control and Autonomous Systems
    Constructive nonlinear control methods for safe stabilization using Lyapunov and barrier functions.

  • Extremum Seeking and Source Seeking for Autonomous Systems
    Nonlinear extremum and source seeking through oscillatory feedback, averaging, and symmetric-product methods.

  • Networked and Underactuated Robotic Systems
    Distributed and cooperative control of heterogeneous, nonholonomic, and underactuated robotic systems.
