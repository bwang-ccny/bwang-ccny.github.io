ME I0000 - Mechanical Engineering Seminars (Fall 2026)

Course Description

ME I0000 is a departmental seminar course featuring invited talks by researchers and professionals in mechanical engineering and related fields. The seminar series provides students with opportunities to learn about current research, emerging technologies, and professional developments across a broad range of topics.
